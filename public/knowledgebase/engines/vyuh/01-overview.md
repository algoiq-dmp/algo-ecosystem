# VYUH — Overview

**Version:** 3.0.0 | **Owner:** Analytics | **Last Updated:** 2026-07-24

## What Is VYUH?

VYUH is a stock analytics and ranking engine that evaluates equities using a combination of technical indicators, fundamental data, and options-derived metrics from TalkOptions. It scores each stock on multiple dimensions — trend strength, relative performance, sector positioning, and volatility characteristics — producing a unified opportunity ranking.

## Why Was It Built?

Traders needed a systematic, data-driven approach to stock selection rather than relying on subjective judgment. VYUH automates the ranking process by consuming real-time and reference data from Surya, Lakshmi, and TalkOptions, ensuring every score is backed by current market data.

## Business Objective

Deliver ranked stock opportunities to Kuber Alpha for strategy activation. Provide DXCC with stock-level analytics for operational oversight and market context.

## Scope

- Multi-factor stock ranking (technical, fundamental, options-derived)
- Trend detection and relative strength analysis
- Sector-level analysis and rotation detection
- Opportunity scoring with configurable weightings
- Signal generation for Kuber Alpha
