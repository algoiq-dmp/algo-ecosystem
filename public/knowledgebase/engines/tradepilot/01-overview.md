# TradePilot — Overview

**Version:** 2.2.0 | **Owner:** Operations | **Last Updated:** 2026-07-24

## What Is TradePilot?

TradePilot is the business and client management platform that serves as the regulatory compliance gateway for the Algo IQ ecosystem. It handles client onboarding with full KYC verification and enforces a mandatory strategy approval workflow that verifies SEBI and exchange regulatory compliance before any strategy can go live.

## Why Was It Built?

SEBI regulations require documented client onboarding and strategy approval processes with complete audit trails. TradePilot was built to automate and enforce these regulatory requirements, ensuring no strategy is deployed without proper compliance verification.

## Business Objective

Ensure every client and strategy meets SEBI and exchange regulatory requirements before going live. Provide a complete approval audit trail for regulatory inspections. Act as the single gateway through which all strategies must pass before reaching Strategy Factory.

## Scope

- Client onboarding with KYC document verification
- Strategy approval workflow with multi-stage review
- SEBI regulatory compliance checks and verification
- Exchange-specific compliance rules enforcement
- Approval documentation and audit trail management
