# 24 — Glossary
> **Version:** 2.0.0 | **Owner:** Data Engineering | **Last Updated:** 2026-07-24
## A
**Accumulation:** Phase where informed participants accumulate positions, typically at low prices with controlled volume.
**Agreement Score:** Percentage of analysis modules pointing in the same directional conclusion.
## B
**Breakout:** Price movement beyond a defined support or resistance level with volume confirmation.
**Breakout Probability:** Manthan's 0-100 score of how likely a breakout is in the near term.
## C
**Climax Volume:** Extremely high single-bar volume (> 3x average), signaling potential exhaustion.
**Compression:** Volatility contraction, measured by Bollinger Band width percentile.
**Confidence Decay:** Natural reduction in confidence score over time without new data.
**Contango:** Short-term volatility < long-term volatility (volatility term structure).
**Cost of Carry:** Futures basis expressed as annualized percentage.
**Cumulative Delta:** Running sum of up-tick minus down-tick volume.
## D
**Distribution:** Phase where informed participants distribute positions, typically at high prices.
**Divergence:** Disagreement between price direction and indicator direction.
**Dry-Up:** Volume consistently below 50% of average.
## I
**Impact Cost:** Price movement caused by executing a hypothetical order of fixed size.
## L
**Liquidity Score:** 0–100 composite score of spread, depth, impact cost, and volume consistency.
**Long Buildup:** Price up + OI up. New long positions being added.
## M
**Market Regime:** Classification of the market environment (Strong Bull, Weak Bull, Sideways, etc.).
**Multi-Timeframe Consistency:** How well signals align across 1m, 5m, 15m, 1h, 1d timeframes.
## O
**OI (Open Interest):** Total outstanding futures/options contracts.
**Order Book Imbalance:** Ratio of bid volume to ask volume in the order book.
## P
**PCR (Put/Call Ratio):** Total put OI / total call OI. Contrarian indicator.
**Price-OI Matrix:** 2x2 grid analyzing price change vs OI change.
## R
**Regime Transition:** Shift from one market regime to another.
**RVOL (Relative Volume):** Current volume / SMA(volume, 20).
**Rollover:** Percentage of contracts carried forward to next expiry.
## S
**Short Covering:** Price up + OI down. Shorts exiting positions.
**Short Buildup:** Price down + OI up. New short positions being added.
**Signal Agreement:** Number of analysis modules that agree on market direction.
## T
**Trend Phase:** Stage of trend lifecycle (Emerging, Accelerating, Mature, Decelerating, Exhausted).
**Transition Probability:** Likelihood of regime change within the next 1-3 bars.
## V
**Volatility Cone:** Range of historical volatility percentiles across tenors.
**Volatility Regime:** Classification of volatility environment (Low, Normal, Elevated, High, Extreme).
## U
**Unwinding:** Long positions being closed (price down + OI down).
