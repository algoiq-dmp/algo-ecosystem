# TalkOffice — Overview

**Version:** 4.0.0 | **Owner:** Operations | **Last Updated:** 2026-07-24

## What Is TalkOffice?

TalkOffice is the unified OMS/RMS platform providing real-time visibility into all trading positions, margins, P&L, and risk exposure. It is the single source of truth for operations teams to monitor broker-wise, strategy-wise, and client-wise positions. All data flows in exclusively through trade confirmations received from Vega.

## Why Was It Built?

Operations teams needed a centralized system to track positions, margins, and risk across multiple brokers and strategies in real time. Before TalkOffice, this required manual reconciliation across disparate broker portals, leading to delays and potential oversight.

## Business Objective

Provide real-time RMS/OMS with position tracking, margin management, live MTM, and exposure monitoring. Serve as the operational backbone for DXCC monitoring and Chitragupta audit trails.

## Scope

- Broker-wise, strategy-wise, client-wise position tracking
- Live MTM and P&L calculation
- Margin utilization and exposure monitoring
- Trade book and order book reconciliation
- Real-time dashboards via WebSocket
