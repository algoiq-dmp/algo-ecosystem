# 24 — Glossary

> **Version:** 4.1.0 | **Owner:** Analytics | **Last Updated:** 2026-07-24

## A

**ADX (Average Directional Index):** Measures trend strength (0–100) without indicating direction. Values > 25 signal a trending market; < 20 signals a range-bound market.

**ATR (Average True Range):** Measures market volatility by decomposing the entire range of an asset price for a given period. Used for position sizing and stop-loss placement.

## B

**Bollinger Bands:** Volatility bands placed above and below a moving average. Volatility is based on standard deviation, which changes as volatility increases and decreases.

**Bollinger Squeeze:** When the bands come close together, constricting the moving average. A precursor to a significant breakout.

**Breakout:** Price movement beyond a defined support or resistance level, often accompanied by increased volume.

**%B:** Measures the position of price relative to Bollinger Bands. %B = 1 when at upper band, 0 at lower band, 0.5 at middle band.

## C

**Camarilla Pivot:** A pivot point variant using a specific set of levels (R1-R4, S1-S4) based on the previous day's range, designed for mean reversion trading.

**Chikou Span:** In Ichimoku, the lagging line plotted 26 periods behind current price. Confirms trend direction.

**Classic Pivot:** The traditional pivot point formula: (H + L + C) / 3, with three support and three resistance levels.

**Composite Score:** Weighted aggregation of all indicator signals into a single -100 to +100 directional score.

**Confluence:** Multiple indicators or methods agreeing on the same signal or level. Higher confluence = higher confidence.

**CPR (Central Pivot Range):** A two-line pivot indicator consisting of the Top Central (TC) and Bottom Central (BC) lines. CPR width signals breakout probability.

**Crossover:** When a faster indicator crosses above or below a slower indicator (e.g., EMA 9 crossing EMA 21).

## D

**Death Cross:** Bearish signal when a short-term moving average crosses below a long-term moving average (typically EMA 50 below EMA 200).

**Divergence:** Discrepancy between price direction and indicator direction. Signals potential trend reversal.

**+DI / -DI:** Positive and Negative Directional Indicators, components of the ADX system that indicate directional movement.

## E

**EMA (Exponential Moving Average):** A weighted moving average that gives more importance to recent price data. Reacts faster to price changes than SMA.

## F

**Fibonacci Retracement:** A technical analysis tool using horizontal lines to indicate areas of support or resistance at key Fibonacci levels (23.6%, 38.2%, 50%, 61.8%, 78.6%).

## G

**Ganesh:** The historical and real-time OHLC data service serving as the primary price data source for Suchak.

**Golden Cross:** Bullish signal when a short-term moving average crosses above a long-term moving average (typically EMA 50 above EMA 200).

## H

**Histogram (MACD):** The difference between the MACD line and the Signal line, visualized as bars. Rising histogram = accelerating momentum.

**HVN (High Volume Node):** A price level with significant traded volume, often acting as strong support or resistance.

## I

**Ichimoku Kinko Hyo:** A comprehensive Japanese indicator providing trend direction, momentum, and support/resistance in a single view using five lines.

## K

**Kijun-sen:** The base line in Ichimoku (26-period midpoint), serving as medium-term trend indicator and strong support/resistance.

**Kumo:** "Cloud" in Japanese; the shaded area between Senkou Span A and B in Ichimoku, representing future support/resistance zones.

## L

**Lakshmi:** The tick-level live market data feed providing real-time bid/ask and last traded price for ultra-low-latency indicator updates.

**LVN (Low Volume Node):** A price level with minimal traded volume, where price tends to move through quickly with little resistance.

## M

**MACD (Moving Average Convergence Divergence):** A trend-following momentum indicator showing the relationship between two moving averages of price.

**Momentum:** The rate of acceleration of a security's price. Measured via ROC, RMI, CMO, and other oscillators.

## N

**NATR (Normalized ATR):** ATR divided by price, expressed as a percentage. Enables cross-symbol volatility comparison.

## O

**OHLC:** Open, High, Low, Close — the four price points defining a candlestick or bar for a given timeframe.

**Overbought:** Condition where price has risen too far, too fast, and may be due for a pullback (RSI > 70, Stochastic > 80).

**Oversold:** Condition where price has fallen too far, too fast, and may be due for a bounce (RSI < 30, Stochastic < 20).

## P

**Pivot Point:** A price level calculated using the previous period's high, low, and close, used to identify potential support and resistance.

**POC (Point of Control):** The price level with the highest traded volume for a given period. The "fairest" price based on market activity.

## R

**ROC (Rate of Change):** A momentum indicator measuring the percentage change in price over a specified period.

**RSI (Relative Strength Index):** A momentum oscillator measuring the speed and magnitude of price changes on a 0–100 scale.

## S

**Senkou Span A/B:** The two lines forming the Ichimoku Cloud, projected 26 periods into the future.

**Signal Line:** The 9-period EMA of the MACD line, used to generate buy/sell crossover signals.

**Signal Strength:** A normalized 0–100 score representing the directional confidence of current market conditions, aggregated from all available indicators.

**SMA (Simple Moving Average):** An unweighted mean of the previous N data points.

**Squeeze:** See Bollinger Squeeze.

**Stochastic Oscillator:** A momentum indicator comparing a closing price to its price range over a given period.

**Support & Resistance:** Price levels where buying or selling pressure historically halted or reversed price movement.

**SuperTrend:** A trend-following indicator overlaying price with a colored line indicating bullish (green) or bearish (red) trend direction.

## T

**Tenkan-sen:** The conversion line in Ichimoku (9-period midpoint), indicating short-term trend direction.

**TK Cross:** Tenkan-sen crossing Kijun-sen in Ichimoku, generating buy/sell signals.

## V

**VWAP (Volume Weighted Average Price):** The average price weighted by volume, used as a trading benchmark and intraday trend indicator.

## Z

**Zero Line Cross:** In MACD, when the MACD line crosses above or below zero, signaling a shift from bearish to bullish or vice versa.
