# TalkDelta AI — Overview

**Version:** 1.4.0 | **Owner:** AI/ML | **Last Updated:** 2026-07-24

## What Is TalkDelta AI?

TalkDelta AI is an AI-powered decision engine that transforms live trading data into actionable, AI-driven insights. It sits downstream of TalkDelta and combines trade execution data with market intelligence from Lakshmi (live prices), Surya (exchange files), and TalkOptions (options analytics) to generate strategy signals and portfolio recommendations.

## Why Was It Built?

Human traders and rule-based strategies cannot process the volume and velocity of market data available. TalkDelta AI applies machine learning models to identify patterns, optimize strategy parameters, and surface opportunities that would otherwise go undetected.

## Business Objective

Generate AI-driven strategy signals and risk insights for Kuber Alpha. Provide automated trade explanations, portfolio intelligence, and opportunity suggestions to augment human decision-making and improve strategy performance.

## Scope

- AI-driven strategy signal generation
- Pattern recognition and anomaly detection
- Strategy optimization recommendations
- Trade explanation and reasoning
- Portfolio-level risk intelligence
