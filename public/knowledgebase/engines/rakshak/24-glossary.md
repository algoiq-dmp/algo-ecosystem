# 24 — Glossary
> **Version:** 2.3.0 | **Owner:** Risk | **Last Updated:** 2026-07-24
## B
**Black Swan:** A rare, unpredictable event with severe consequences. Rakshak's disaster protection layer is designed for these.
## C
**Circuit Breaker:** Exchange-mandated trading halt when prices move beyond predefined limits (10%, 15%, 20% for NIFTY).
**Conditional VaR (CVaR):** Average loss beyond the VaR threshold. Better measure of tail risk than VaR alone.
**Concentration Risk:** Overexposure to a single underlying, sector, or strategy type.
**Correlation Breakdown:** When normally correlated instruments decouple, reducing hedge effectiveness.
## D
**Disaster Protection:** Rakshak's final defense layer against systemic failures and black swan events.
**Dynamic Hedging:** Continuous adjustment of hedge ratios based on changing market conditions.
## E
**Emergency Exit:** Rapid, automated liquidation of positions when risk exceeds critical thresholds.
**Event Calendar:** Database of scheduled events (RBI, earnings, expiry) with impact assessments.
**Expected Shortfall:** See CVaR.
## G
**Gap Risk:** Potential loss from price gaps between market close and next open.
## H
**Hedge Efficiency:** Ratio of risk reduction to hedge cost. Minimum 2.0 required by Rakshak.
**Hedge Ratio:** Size of hedge relative to position size. Dynamic hedge ratio adjusts in real-time.
## M
**Maximum Adverse Excursion (MAE):** Largest historical loss in similar market conditions.
## O
**Overnight Risk:** Risk specific to holding positions when markets are closed and unhedgeable.
## P
**Portfolio Protection Score:** 0-100 composite of concentration, correlation, and stress loss risks.
**Pre-Trade Hedge Check:** Rakshak validation of hedge adequacy before a position is entered.
## S
**Stress Test:** Simulation of extreme market scenarios to estimate portfolio impact.
## T
**Tail Risk:** Exposure to rare, extreme market moves (the "fat tails" of the return distribution).
**Tail Hedge:** Options-based protection against extreme moves (OTM puts, VIX calls).
## V
**Value at Risk (VaR):** Maximum expected loss at a given confidence level over a specified time period.
**VIX Hedge:** Using volatility futures/options as a portfolio hedge (volatility spikes during crashes).
